# Images of the gallery

`examples/gallery.rs` draws one image of each format that an asset takes.

`dice.png` is the 250 pixel thumbnail of
[PNG transparency demonstration 1.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PNG_transparency_demonstration_1.png)
by Ed g2s, Daniel G. and CyberShadow, under CC BY-SA 3.0. It has an alpha
channel.

`landscape.jpg` is
[Landscape_6.jpg](https://github.com/recurser/exif-orientation-examples),
copyright (c) 2010 Dave Perrett, under the MIT license, scaled to 160 by
240 pixels. The pixels are stored on their side, and the EXIF orientation 6 turns them
upright.

`horse.gif` is
[Animhorse.gif](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Animhorse.gif) by
Janke, under CC BY-SA 2.5. It has eight frames, and a view shows the first.

`cat.webp` is
[A Siamese cat.webp](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_Siamese_cat.webp),
under CC0. It is a lossy WebP.
